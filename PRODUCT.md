# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters hiring senior PMs or founders looking for a co-founder / senior PM. They scan fast and are drowning in AI-generated candidate sites. They're looking for signal: proof of real work, real judgment, real range. They don't have time for "passionate problem-solvers."

## Product Purpose

Hadar Sapir's personal website. Not a resume in disguise — a living proof of what he does. The goal is to make one thing land: this person ships, handles everything, and does it with both data and people. Someone who sees this site should leave with a specific impression, not a vague "seems solid."

## Brand Personality

Decisive, human, data-native. Action over adjectives. Warm but not soft. Specific because vague is forgettable.

## Anti-references

- AI-generated portfolio sites: SaaS-cream backgrounds, purple gradient text everywhere, "Passionate problem-solver with 10+ years of experience driving cross-functional alignment" copy
- Cookie-cutter bento templates where every card is the same size and says nothing specific
- Gradient-text hero names and glowing metric numbers with no substance behind them
- Stock-photo vibes — anything that could belong to anyone
- Over-designed personal brand aesthetics that feel like a startup marketing page, not a person

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — every claim earns a proof point. Metrics beat adjectives. A git log beats a bullet list.
2. **Human precision** — data without warmth is a spreadsheet; warmth without data is fluff. Both together is rare and memorable.
3. **Specific over polished** — a real travel story beats a beautiful placeholder. A real terminal card beats a hero gradient.
4. **Earned distinctiveness** — stand out because the work is specific, not because the design is loud. The site should feel handmade, not generated.
5. **Action as identity** — the design itself demonstrates doing: interactive, detailed, clearly built by someone who cares about craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Existing `prefers-reduced-motion` support should be preserved. Focus-visible states maintained on all interactive elements.
